Detective Advice: 3 Rules for Booking Your Paris Date Night

1
Aim for the 20:30 Departure (Sunset + Night Illumination):

The 20:30 evening sailing gives you the magical transition of seeing the historic Paris bridges at sunset, followed by the dramatic monuments illuminated in golden spotlights after dark.

2
Coordinate With The Eiffel Tower Sparkles:

After dusk, the Eiffel Tower sparkles for exactly 5 minutes at the beginning of every hour (21:00, 22:00, 23:00). On Bateaux Mouches, the captain times the route so the boat is positioned right in front of the tower at the hour mark.
