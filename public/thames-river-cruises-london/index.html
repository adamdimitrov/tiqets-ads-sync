10 Best Thames River Cruises in London (2026 Ranked & Tested)

Last updated: March 2026 • Personally Field-Tested Undercover

Author: Adam Dimitrov — Chief Cruise Detective • Personally Field-Tested

With tidal currents shifting up to 7 meters twice daily and iconic bridges framing the Houses of Parliament, Tower Bridge, and Greenwich, the River Thames is London's true historic spine. But choosing the wrong cruise can turn a scenic milestone into an overcrowded commute.

We personally tested and benchmarked all 10 major Thames river cruise options—from budget sightseeing boats to 50-knot high-speed rigid inflatable speedboats and sunset jazz dinners—to help you pick the perfect voyage for your London itinerary.
