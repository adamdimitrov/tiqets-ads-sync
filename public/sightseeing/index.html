About the Bateaux Parisiens Experience:

Recognized as the flagship Seine River cruise operator in Paris, Bateaux Parisiens operates purpose-built double-decker trimarans designed specifically for panoramic sightseeing. Departing directly from the foot of the Eiffel Tower, the 70-minute voyage takes you through the historical heart of Paris along the UNESCO World Heritage-listed riverbanks.

Key Landmarks Seen Along the 70-Minute Route:

1 Eiffel Tower & Champ de Mars — Departure & return point at Pier 3
2 Les Invalides — The golden dome where Napoleon Bonaparte rests
3 Musée d'Orsay — The legendary Beaux-Arts railway station museum
4 Île de la Cité & Notre-Dame Cathedral — Gothic masterpiece on the river island
5 Conciergerie & Sainte-Chapelle — Medieval royal palace and prison
6 Hôtel de Ville — Paris’s grand neo-Renaissance city hall
7 The Louvre Museum — The world’s largest art museum stretching along the bank
8 Place de la Concorde & Grand Palais — Monumental squares and Belle Époque glass domes
9 Historic Seine Bridges — Pont Alexandre III, Pont Neuf, Pont des Arts and Pont Royal

Local Tip for Evening Departures:

Boats departing 30 minutes before sunset offer the best of both worlds: Parisian monuments glowing in the golden twilight on the outbound leg, and the Eiffel Tower sparkling in brilliant diamond white lights on your return (sparkling occurs for 5 minutes at the top of every hour after dusk).
