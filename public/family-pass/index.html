Frequently Asked Family Questions

Do we have to book a specific departure time? ▾

No! Tickets are open-dated and fully flexible. Boats depart every 30 to 45 minutes between 10:00 AM and 10:30 PM. If your kids need a snack or nap, you can take any departure that suits your family schedule.

Can we bring strollers and prams on board? ▾

Yes, absolutely. Bateaux Parisiens has gentle ramps down to Pier 3, wide gangways, and dedicated space on board to keep your stroller or folding pram during the cruise at no extra charge.

How do we receive our tickets? ▾

Your tickets arrive instantly via email and SMS as digital smartphone barcodes. You do not need to print anything—simply display them on your phone at the Pier 3 turnstiles.

What happens if the weather turns cold or rainy? ▾

Bateaux Parisiens ships feature a heated, climate-controlled glass canopy salon downstairs with panoramic views, as well as an open 360° upper sun deck. It is completely comfortable in any weather.

How does infant admission work? ▾

Infants aged 0 to 3 cruise 100% free of charge. You can simply bring them along without purchasing an extra ticket.
