THE HIGH-STAKES TRAVEL REALITY

Vacations Don't Have A Return Policy

Why generic travel blogs and OTA reviews fail travelers when real money and romantic evenings are on the line.

The Missing Safety Net

When a flawed gadget arrives from an online shop, you send it back for a full refund. But when you spend €300 on an anniversary Seine dinner cruise and spend two hours trapped at a cramped center table behind steamed-up windows with lukewarm catering, you cannot buy back your evening.

The PR & Influencer Blindspot

Influencers rarely tell the truth because their business model depends on free comped tickets and VIP champagne greetings. If they criticize a 45-minute boarding bottleneck or tough duck confit, PR agencies drop them immediately. We accept zero freebies.

OTA Rating Distortions

Online travel agencies (OTAs) take 20–25% commissions from operators and bundle ratings across vastly different time slots. A 5-star rating for a sunset glass of wine on a quiet Tuesday does not reflect the cramped chaos of an 8:30 PM Saturday dinner seating.

EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE

Our Five Non-Negotiable Ethics

How we maintain total commercial and editorial independence.

01

100% Anonymous Booking

We never alert cruise operators that a review is taking place. We reserve under personal names, board in general guest queues, and receive the identical service any paying traveler experiences.

02

100% Out-of-Pocket Payment

Every ticket, meal upgrade, champagne package, and audio guide is paid out of pocket. We reject free press passes, media comps, and corporate junkets.

03

Galley & Seating Audits

We evaluate whether dishes are prepared fresh in an onboard kitchen with active chefs or simply reheated from off-site banquet catering. We audit table spacing, acoustic music levels, and window visibility.

04

Zero Pay-for-Rank Placements

No operator can pay to appear as the "#1 Editor's Pick" or modify our candid critiques. Rankings are determined entirely by food quality, vessel comfort, and verified value for money.

05

Price-Transparency Anchoring

We disclose all hidden costs: window seat surcharges, beverage package caps, cloakroom fees, and boarding pier walking distances.
