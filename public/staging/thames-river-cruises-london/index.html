London Cruise Itinerary

Orange = Meeting Points, Colored Layers = Itineraries

Thames River cruise companies generally start from Westminster Pier and offer two disembarking points at either Tower Bridge Pier or Greenwich. Depending on the ticket tier, you have the option to remain on board and return to the starting point.

Along the way, you'll have the chance to explore sights such as:

  • Cutty Sark
  • Tower of London
  • St. Paul’s Cathedral

  • Houses of Parliament
  • Westminster Bridge
  • Royal Festival Hall
  • Cleopatra's Needle
  • Somerset House
  • Millennium Footbridge
  • Shakespeare's Globe Monument
  • H.M.S. Belfast

Summary

💡❓ Is it possible to reserve seats on Thames river cruises? Seats on virtually all river cruises are assigned on a first-come, first-served basis.

Thames Rockets is one of the leading speedboat companies in London, so naturally I had to test them.

If you're up for an adrenaline-packed adventure, London's speedboats are a must-try.

These sleek vessels zoom along the Thames at thrilling speeds of up to 30 knots (55 km/h), making them perfect for those who find traditional sightseeing cruises too sedate.

You'll stay (sort of) dry and very safe thanks to the waterproof jackets provided and metal guard rails to hold onto.

(Not that you'll ever be in danger of flying out of the boat, haha.)

Your journey starts from the London Eye Waterloo Pier, heading down to Greenwich before returning to the starting point. Along the way, you'll switch between leisurely cruising and heart-pounding speedboat mode. Their ride lasts a comfortable 50 minutes.

Before the Cruise

Waiting Area

  • This cruise kicks off at the the London Eye Waterloo Pier, right next to the London Eye. The boarding area is shared with other companies and has an air-conditioned, weather-proof waiting area with vending machines. Thames Rockets is at the very end of the pier.

Safety

  • Inflatable life jackets are provided in case of an emergency. Nothing too surprising here.
  • Onboard, you will be seated in a comfortable padded seat with metal handles to hold. Even during the speedy parts, there was never any real danger of falling out or hitting yourself during a hard break.

Getting There

  • 🗺️ Meeting point: The London Eye Millennium Pier, London SE1 7PB, United Kingdom

During The Cruise

The Ship

  • Thames Rocket's speedboat has a 12-person capacity, with comfortable seats equipped with safety handes to hold onto.

Cruise Itinerary

  • Starting at London Eye Pier, this cruise takes passengers down to Canary Wharf and back.

Live Commentary by Skipper

  • The cruise offers live English commentary in a cheeky, English humor style, making the tour both entertaining and informative.

Slow vs. Speedy Parts

  • The initial part of the cruise proceeds at an enjoyable, leisurely pace, accompanied by live guided commentary, until the boat passes the Tower Bridge. Then the fun begins!
  • Once we crossed Tower Bridge, the skipper finally put the pedal to the metal! It was thrilling to speed along the Thames with the beautiful British cityscape all around us. This part lasts until Greenwich, with short breaks between the high-speed periods.
  • This a fun activity especially for families and with friends.

Thames Ribs is another premier speedboat operator in London, offering an exhilarating way to experience the city's iconic river.

For those seeking a heart-racing adventure on the water, Thames Ribs delivers an unforgettable ride at impressise speeds, similar to Thames Rockets.

Again, safety is a top priority, with life jackets provided to keep you from meeting the Great Almighty before your time. Sturdy handrails offer additional peace of mind as you zoom along the river's twists and turns.

Depending on your chosen experience, your aquatic journey launches from either Embankment Pier or Tower Bridge Pier.

Thames Ribs offers two speedboat options: an extended 70-minute cruise which includes a visit to the Thames Barrier, or a condensed 40-minute ride for a quick but intense Thames adventure until Greenwich.

The tour alternates between calm cruising and pulse-pounding high-speed runs. During these faster segments, prepare for thrilling sideways maneuvers that amp up the excitement and give you a true taste of the boat's capabilities.

An engaging guide provides live commentary throughout the trip, sharing fascinating tidbits about London's riverside landmarks.

Waiting Area

Safety

  • Inflatable life jackets are provided in case of an emergency. Metal safety handles are located at every seat that you can hold onto, unless you want to go for a swim in the Thames.

Getting There

Cruise Itinerary

Live Commentary by Skipper

Slow vs. Speedy Parts

Summary & Review — How Was It and What to Expect?

Explore London like never before with the Hop-On Hop-Off 1 Day River Pass on the Uber Boat by Thames Clippers. This pass lets you travel on the fastest, most frequent fleet of high-speed catamarans, connecting major attractions, monuments, and historic sites along the river.

Cruise in comfort, with climate-controlled indoor cabins, a covered outdoor deck, and licensed coffee bars. Your 1-day pass gives you unlimited access to all stops, allowing you to take in top London sights, from the London Eye and Tower of London to Shakespeare’s Globe and the Shard.

Boats depart every 10-20 minutes from key locations like Battersea Power Station, Westminster, London Bridge, and Greenwich, making it easy to explore the city at your own pace.

The experience is family-friendly too—kids go free with the Family Pass, and children under 5 travel for free.

Summary & Review — How Was It and What to Expect?

This 3-hour evening cruise offers a unique way to experience London as its famous landmarks light up at night. Departing from Westminster, the cruise heads towards the Thames Barrier and returns, with a welcome drink to start your journey, followed by a 4-course dinner.

As you pass iconic landmarks like the O2 Arena, Canary Wharf, and City Hall, you’ll enjoy live music from a singer performing a blend of classic and contemporary hits. Feel free to dance along or simply relax and take in the music and stunning views of the city’s illuminated sights.

The cruise ends back at Westminster, offering a perfect evening of delicious food, live entertainment, and breathtaking views of London.
